## Genuine external verification remaining

- Physical Android 15 permission UX, real sensors/audio/vibration, external/document-provider diversity, removable media and background/resume behavior.
- Interactive Windows testing across devices/ACLs/audio/Wi-Fi drivers and native share destinations.
- Startup/frame time, target-hardware memory/CPU and sustained battery profiling.
- Production Android key and optional Windows certificate; the delivered packages explicitly disclose fallback signing.

## Deliberate platform boundaries

No system-wide Android CPU percentage, invented battery wear, unrestricted Android storage, ICMP claims, guaranteed LAN discovery or arbitrary overall health score. Provider encrypted ZIP and unsupported Windows vibration/sensors have explanatory states. PDF shaping remains subject to font/library support. These boundaries follow platform capabilities and measurement methodology.
